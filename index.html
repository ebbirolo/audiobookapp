Kindle Transcript Guide

How to Get Text from Kindle for This App

Since this PWA cannot directly access the Kindle app due to iOS/Android sandboxing restrictions, follow these steps to get text from your Kindle book:

  1. Prepare Your Kindle Book:
    • Open the Kindle app on your device
    • Navigate to the book you want to convert
    • Go to the beginning of the section you want to capture
  2. Enable Scroll Mode (if available):
    • Some Kindle versions have a "Scroll" or "Continuous Scrolling" mode in settings
    • Enable this if available for smoother capture
  3. Capture the Text:
    • Use your device's screenshot function to capture pages of text
    • For iOS: Press Side Button + Volume Up simultaneously
    • For Android: Press Power + Volume Down simultaneously
    • Capture each page, making sure to get all the text
  4. Extract Text from Screenshots:
    • Use an OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool to convert images to text
    • Free online options: Google Keep, Microsoft OneNote, or free OCR websites
    • Mobile apps: Adobe Scan, Microsoft Lens, or Google Photos (with Google Lens)
  5. Prepare the Text File:
    • Copy the extracted text into a plain text editor
    • Clean up any OCR errors (misrecognized characters, missing spaces, etc.)
    • Save as a .txt file
    • Note: For DRM-protected books, this is for personal use only
  6. Use in This App:
    • Return to the main screen of this app
    • Tap "Load File" and select your .txt file
    • Or paste the text directly into the textarea
    • Adjust volume, rate, and pitch as desired
    • Tap "Play" to listen

Important Notes:

• This process is for personal use only with books you own

• Do not distribute or share copyrighted text without permission

• The quality depends on your OCR tool and cleanup effort

• For best results, work in small chunks (10-20 pages at a time)
